# Post-match summary and rematches

Every completed Casual, Competitive and Private match uses the same native presentation, including Knockout 1v1/2v2/3v3 and BOB. Immediately after the final replay, one visual-only copy of each winning player's equipped puck is staged at arena center, with their name and contributions beneath it. The five-second showcase transitions through a 1.4-second camera pullback into a left menu and right scoreboard. Continue starts the pullback early. The scoreboard is the existing Tab widget, embedded without its fullscreen scrim; it uses self-KO and switch columns after the match instead of live shot/survivor columns. The arena stays visible, and no gameplay puck transforms, collisions or physics parameters are changed. Local copies and temporary render hiding are cleaned up on rematch, lineup selection, leaving the game or controller teardown.

The summary lists every player, opponent knockouts, own-team losses caused during their shot, and switches physically activated by their pucks. Switches and self-knockouts add no points. There is no individual MVP, most-shots or most-impacts award. BOB uses pocketed pucks and striker penalties and displays switches as unavailable. Winner cards show contributions rather than ranking teammates. Forfeits explicitly have no decisive shot.

Public network matches need consent from every original seat. A departed player does not reduce the required roster. Play Again preserves teams, classes and custom lineups; Change Lineup requests the same rematch with class selection before play. Either request marks that player ready. The existing class-selection deadline still applies. Solo/local matches start immediately and preserve the other local classes when the player edits their own class. Canceling a local lineup edit keeps the original lineup and starts the already-accepted rematch, rather than returning to a completed match whose pucks were removed.

Private matches are host-led. Same-lineup rematches retain room settings, controlled slots and bot seats. Change Roles / Lineup reopens the existing private role and class selection flow. Guests remain in the room and wait for the host.

Competitive results must settle before a new result-bearing match is registered. Every rematch gets a fresh ID and authoritative roster. Coordinator allocations transfer process ownership to the successor match, so retiring a completed match cannot terminate its rematch. Previous result records remain immutable. The public coordinator rematch window defaults to 75 seconds (`PublicRematchTimeLimit`, bounded to 5-80 seconds); this fits within the coordinator's 90-second completed-server retirement. Private/listen/local matches do not have this deadline. A rejected registration leaves the summary available with an error and permits retry.

Validation: `FLICK.Match.PostMatchSummaryAndRematch` covers context counters, series reset and full-roster voting for all supported team sizes/variants. `FLICK.Match.PostMatchArenaPresentation` covers one collision-free non-replicated display puck per winner, preserved gameplay transforms and cleanup for 1v1/2v2/3v3. `FLICK.Match.PostMatchRematchFlow` covers actual local/private restarts, preserved classes and room rules, new result IDs, private role selection and local lineup cancellation across the supported formats. `Tools/Test-FlickCoordinator.ps1` covers continuation allocations and competitive registration/settlement. `Tools/Capture-FlickUI.ps1 -Screen PostMatch,PostMatchPrivate,PostMatchBob,PostMatchRanked -CaptureDelaySeconds 8` captures the summary; use a delay below five seconds for the winner presentation.
