# Switchyard arena presentation

The new design is permanent for 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3. Guides and rim accents scale
with arena radius; socket frames follow the authoritative layout and actual
divider lengths. BOB is unchanged.

The old-design toggle, material rollback cache and original-design capture flag
have been removed. Existing imported assets remain necessary source geometry
and material parents, not a separate backup map.

All added dressing is non-colliding. Gameplay dimensions, layouts, friction,
restitution, switch timing and puck physics are unchanged.

Menu and gameplay now share the 250 softbox multiplier, light shapes/colours,
camera-relative reflections, deck finish and Switchyard colour grade. Gameplay
does not enable menu depth of field. Menu and Gameplay use one shared preference
preset. Existing menu values are retained as the shared settings; legacy gameplay
values are ignored. Changes from either screen apply to both.

The key and warm rectangular sources are broad panels (1000x550 cm and
650x500 cm at the 1v1 radius), scaled with each arena. They illuminate from
beyond the far rim so their reflections do not wash across the near half of the
playing field. The shared power multiplier remains 250. Source areas and
positions soften/localize the highlights. The source frame follows the viewing
azimuth with the cool source opposite the camera, keeping its reflection near
the far-center instead of bleaching the left half of the deck. Dimensions are tuneable in the lighting
rig's `FParameters`.

## Architectural finish

The deck now uses dark, neutral brushed metal rather than emissive pale blue.
`M_ArchitecturalMetal` adds subtle world-space machining/handling variation to
base colour and roughness, with derivative-filtered brushing to avoid distant
shimmer. Warped, overlapping wear varies the deck's roughness rather than drawing
one bright scratch per regular cell; occasional fine scratches remain subdued.
Filtered sub-millimetre world-space normals break up
real reflected light rather than painting broad cloudy patches into the surface.
The deck uses 0.18 roughness and the tuneable `OneVsOneDeckTint` gunmetal
reflectance (default linear RGB 0.18/0.215/0.25; near-black
metal base colours absorb reflections); ambient environment intensity is reduced
to keep contrast while the softboxes/stadium provide localized highlights.
The same parent finishes the Knockout stadium floor, seating and stairs.
It is generated by `Tools/Build-FlickArchitecturalMaterial.py`; no texture maps,
FBX reimports, mesh changes or new collision are needed. Existing render-layer
offsets are copied exactly, preserving the previous anti-flicker fixes. The main
deck explicitly evaluates those offsets in hardware ray tracing too: otherwise
hidden, unshifted deck/inlay faces can block the reflection rays above the visible
surface. This changes ray-tracing presentation only, never the physics mesh.

The rim's graphite sidewall gets 28 broad amber segments with brighter inner
cores, masked on its existing faces. The cyan top lenses remain clearly luminous.
All switch colours, socket indicators and puck materials stay unchanged.

The collar has a restrained convex normal response and a polished metal finish;
this is shading, not displacement. Rounded cyan lens masks extend across the
existing collar (6 cm at 1v1 scale), aligned with the authored 28 inset lenses,
with pale cores and dark gaskets. Radius, lens count and emission update on cached
materials when formats or presentation settings change. Derivative-filtered edges
avoid introducing sub-pixel shimmer. Neither vertices nor render-layer offsets move.
Only the material lenses render when the architectural parent is available:
the former near-coplanar instanced lenses/bezels are retained as a missing-material
fallback, not drawn over the shader. Polished rim normals use the smooth crown
without the deck's fine bumps, and pixel-area coverage filters the cyan edges.
This removes the duplicated depth/shading boundary without changing the collar
mesh, silhouette or collision. Lens emission defaults to 30; amber cores have
lower peak radiance to reduce clipping and unstable tiny reflected highlights.
`IndirectEmissionScale` limits these architectural LEDs to 15% radiance in
ray-traced materials and Lumen surface-cache captures, while the visible raster
lenses remain full strength. The explicit local sources supply the stable spill.
This is a material-local budget for small HDR emitters, not a global reflection
or Lumen-quality reduction; puck, switch and other materials are unaffected.
The Switchyard camera uses Lumen scene traces consistently for reflections and
final-gather lighting instead of mixing in screen-color/depth hits from its thin
bright edges. Lumen itself remains enabled, and hardware/software tracing follows
the player's existing settings. These per-view overrides are removed outside
Switchyard (including BOB), rather than changing global renderer CVars.

Four tagged, shadow-free rectangular lights align with the existing seating banks
and provide restrained warm floor spill. They scale with arena area, share the
warm-light/reflection preferences and are reused on every settings/format update.
They are disabled outside Knockout; BOB materials/geometry are untouched.
`FlickArenaLighting::FParameters::StadiumWarmLightLumens` tunes this spill.

Twelve additional tagged, shadow-free capsule sources provide the **near-rim**
amber wash. Visible emissive strips alone were too small on the large arena mesh
to illuminate this area reliably through Lumen. The sources sit 5 cm outside the
skirt, 32 cm above the concourse, run tangentially along local Z and have a
450 cm maximum range at 1v1 scale. Soft-source radii and restrained specular
response prevent detached glowing bars in the floor. The concourse uses 0.27
roughness with a partial metallic response so it receives both soft light and
reflections; 140 cm tangent sources and the slightly higher origin extend the
amber reflection along the floor rather than concentrating it into short spots.
The deck keeps its separate reflective-metal finish.

`FParameters::RimSpillLightLumens` (32 lm per source) tunes this near-rim wash.
Positions/ranges scale with radius and power with board area. These local,
non-replicated lights are reused across menu/match/format/settings changes, follow
the shared warm-light/reflection preferences, are disabled outside Knockout, and
are never created on dedicated servers. Rim proxies contribute direct light only,
avoiding an additional stochastic GI bounce. No dynamic shadow maps or volumetric
effects are added. BOB remains unchanged.

The architectural grade removes film grain, softens contrast/AO, tightens bloom
and modestly increases Lumen reflection quality. It does not force hardware ray
tracing or raise global scalability; lower-quality configurations retain their
existing rendering path. Saved lighting preferences are never overwritten.
