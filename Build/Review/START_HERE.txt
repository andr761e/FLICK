FLICK: KNOCKOUT // DEVELOPMENT BUILD
PLAYTEST INSTRUCTIONS // COFFEE STAIN GROUP

Thank you for taking a look at FLICK: Knockout!

FLICK is a physics-first competitive game about aim, shot power, positioning
and decision-making. It is deliberately more measured than a reflex-heavy
action game: small moves can create large consequences. Puck skins and other
cosmetics do not change gameplay attributes.


// 1. DOWNLOAD AND LAUNCH

1. Download the complete ZIP from Google Drive.
2. Right-click the ZIP and choose "Extract All". Do not launch from inside
   the ZIP preview.
3. Open the extracted Windows folder and double-click FLICK.exe.
4. Keep the Engine and FLICK subfolders and steam_appid.txt beside the
   executable. Do not move or share the executable on its own.

This is a Windows development build. Unreal Engine, a development environment
and a Steam key are not required to run the packaged game.

For Steam invitations/private online matches, open Steam and sign in before
launching FLICK. Offline activities do not require matchmaking servers.

If a Windows security warning appears, only continue if you have verified that
this is the review build supplied by the developer. Do not disable antivirus
or firewall protection. If Windows reports missing runtime components, contact
the developer for help rather than downloading DLLs from third-party sites.


// 2. SUGGESTED FIRST PLAYTHROUGH (ABOUT 10-15 MINUTES)

1. PLAY > TRAINING > TUTORIAL: learn aiming, power, knockouts and switches.
2. PLAY > TRAINING > 1V1 VS BOT: try a complete Knockout match. Start with
   a lower bot difficulty, then increase it as you get comfortable.
3. Try BOB VS BOT for the alternative pocketing ruleset.
4. Explore PRACTICE PACKS, LINEUPS and PROFILE/customization.

You can evaluate the core game alone. A second PC is only needed to test
online private matches with another player.


// 3. HOW TO PLAY

Knockout:
- Knock your opponent's pucks off the arena while keeping yours in play.
- A standard 1v1 match gives each player four pucks. Win three rounds to
  win the match under the default rules.
- Each round begins with simultaneous kickoff planning: both sides lock
  in their opening shot, then those shots launch together.
- After kickoff, players take turns. Let the physics resolve before the
  next shot. Follow the turn indicator and on-screen prompts.
- Crossing a colored switch changes its linked divider. Dividers affect
  shot routes and can launch or flip pucks. Positioning matters.
- Different puck types have different physical strengths and trade-offs.
  Use LINEUPS to inspect them and choose your four-puck lineup.

Taking a shot:
- Hold the left mouse button on one of your selectable pucks.
- Pull back opposite the direction you want to shoot.
- Drag farther for more power, then release to launch.
- Use the aiming preview to judge the direction and likely contact.

BOB:
- Use your striker to pocket your own colored pucks before your opponent
  clears theirs. The board has four corner pockets.
- Turns alternate after shots resolve. Avoid pocketing your own striker:
  it is returned and can bring back one of your previously pocketed pucks
  as a penalty.


// 4. OFFLINE ACTIVITIES (PLAY > TRAINING)

TUTORIAL
Four guided lessons covering aiming, power, knockouts and switches.

FREE PLAY
Experiment without an opponent or build your own shot setup. Choose a
Knockout arena size or BOB, edit the board, then retry shots. Backspace
undoes the last Free Play shot; R resets your saved setup.

1V1 VS BOT
A complete offline Knockout series against an AI opponent. Change BOT
DIFFICULTY in the Training menu before starting.

BOB VS BOT
An offline BOB match against an AI opponent, using the pocketing rules.

PRACTICE PACKS
Choose Precision, Knockouts, Switch Control or BOB Pockets, then Foundation,
Skilled or Expert difficulty. Each pack contains five scored challenges.
Aim for five out of five; personal bests are saved locally. In Precision,
the entire puck must finish inside the marked circle.


// 5. PRIVATE MATCHES WITH A FRIEND THROUGH STEAM

Use separate Windows PCs and separate Steam accounts that are Steam friends.
Each player needs the same complete build. Multiple windows on one PC with
one Steam account are not a substitute for this setup.

1. Open Steam and sign in on every PC, then launch FLICK on each PC.
2. The host opens SOCIAL in FLICK and invites their friend to the party.
3. The friend accepts the invitation. Confirm you are together in the party.
4. The party leader selects PLAY > PRIVATE MATCH and configures the match.
5. Players choose available team slots (or spectate) and ready up.
6. Once all required playing slots are filled and ready, the host starts.

Start with 1v1 for two players. Larger team formats need enough players to
fill their playing slots; empty slots are not automatically filled by bots.
Private matches are hosted by the party leader's PC. The host must remain
in the game; leaving as host ends the current match. Private results do
not count toward Competitive MMR.

No separate server command, VPN or public matchmaking service is needed
for the intended Steam private-match flow. If invitations do not work,
check that Steam is online on both PCs, both have the same build, and the
game is permitted through Windows Firewall. Offline modes remain available.


// 6. USEFUL DEFAULT CONTROLS

Left mouse button       Hold on your puck, pull back and release to shoot
Escape                  Pause / back / access settings
Mouse wheel             Adjust camera height/zoom
Q / E                   Orbit the camera
V                       Top view
F                       Reset camera
Tab                     Scoreboard
Middle mouse button     Ping a puck or switch
F1-F4                   Quick chat groups/choices
Enter                   Skip a cinematic replay

Free Play:
T                       Toggle the board editor
1 / 2                   Place your own / target puck while editing
Delete                  Remove a puck while editing
Backspace               Undo your last Free Play shot
R                       Reset the training setup

Controls can be rebound under SETTINGS > CONTROLS. Other settings include
sound/music, camera, display, lighting, interface and quick-chat preferences.
Follow the on-screen hints when switching between gameplay and editing.


// 7. WHAT IS NOT LIVE IN THIS BUILD

- CASUAL and COMPETITIVE public matchmaking are not available for this
  review build. Public servers have not been deployed. Please use Training
  or Steam private matches instead of searching those playlists.
- The ITEM SHOP is a demonstration/placeholder, not a real-money store.
- Rank/MMR displays and development testing controls are not evidence of
  a live ranked population or a production competitive backend.
- This is an in-development build, not a finished release. Bugs, balancing
  issues and unfinished presentation may still be encountered.

PROFILE/customization can be explored to see puck colors, trails, spawn
and knockout effects, banners and other cosmetic presentation options.
