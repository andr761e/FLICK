Badge design language: original precision-engineered insignia, premium machined metal with clean bevels, restrained emissive inlays, bold instantly distinguishable silhouettes and clean negative spaces, readable as small game HUD icons. Common small puck-disc core with a circular center cutout. No crowns, no wings, no lions, no shields copied from other competitive games. Lower ranks simple, higher ranks progressively prestigious but still clear, not overloaded with filigree. Neutral studio rim light, extremely crisp polished asset-design rendering, no fog or distracting particles.
Progression: each row must retain a recognizable shared family silhouette. Level I is the clean base form; II adds a restrained structural layer; III adds a clearly stronger outer frame. Each level ALSO has exactly one, two or three short illuminated division marks integrated along the lower edge. Do not make three identical icons with different text.
CARBON: charcoal carbon-composite core, one sturdy open-bottom geometric chevron around a puck, silver-white edge accents; deliberate and simple. II/III add nested supporting chevrons.
ALLOY: brushed cool steel, two interlocking machined segments enclosing the core; II/III reinforce the interlocking architecture, no gold.
TEMPERED: warm copper-orange, forged angular triangular frame with three inward points around the core; increasingly reinforced precision metal.
VECTOR: electric cyan and dark titanium, four precise directional arrow-blades aiming toward the core; develop a confident four-point compass silhouette, not a snowflake.
PRISM: iridescent blue-violet crystal, distinct faceted angular asymmetric outer crystal frame enclosing the puck core, progressively more refined separated crystal facets. Delicate iridescence, clear silhouette.
OVERDRIVE: vivid crimson with graphite and silver, split circular core frame and swept-back acceleration fins, mechanical high-speed energy, no literal bird wings.
SINGULARITY: dark violet and bright white, dark circular core suspended within an angular fractured orbital frame, layers of controlled energy; prestigious and visually separate from Overdrive.
ZENITH: pinnacle badge only, singular distinctive sharp symmetrical six-point architectural star, pearl-white machined metal with small lime emissive accents and brilliant central puck core; largest and most prestigious, generous breathing room, no Roman numeral, no rank number. White/lime rather than gold.
Text: exact labels above, small but legible uppercase geometric sans serif, evenly centered below each badge; labels outside badge art. No MMR ranges, no other ranks, no extra icons, no watermark. Maintain consistent visual scale in the first 21 cells and consistent lighting. Produce a polished coherent sheet that a game art team can approve before extracting individual assets.
